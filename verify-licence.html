What is CM/L?

A Certification Marks Licence (CM/L) number is a 7 to 10 digit unique identifier granted to manufacturing plants meeting standard specifications.

Understanding 6-Digit HUID

Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) is an alphanumeric code laser-engraved by recognized assaying centres certifying gold purity.

Found a Counterfeit?

Consumers can lodge instant vigilance complaints for fake ISI marks or purity shortfalls under the BIS Act 2016.
