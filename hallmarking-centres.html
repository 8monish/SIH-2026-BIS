The 3 Mandatory Signs on Authentic BIS Hallmarked Gold

1. BIS Standard Logo

Official triangular hallmark stamp signifying National Standards compliance.

2. Purity / Fineness Grade

22K916 (91.6% Pure) | 18K750 (75.0%) | 14K585 (58.5%)

3. 6-Digit HUID

Unique alphanumeric laser code (e.g. AB1234) verifiable on the BIS CARE App.
